# Types of Artificial Intelligence

## Introduction

Artificial Intelligence is not a single technology. Instead, it consists of different systems built to solve different kinds of problems.

Some AI systems perform only one specific task extremely well, while researchers continue working toward creating AI that can perform many different tasks like a human being.

Understanding these different types of AI helps us appreciate both the capabilities and current limitations of Artificial Intelligence.

---

## Narrow AI (Artificial Narrow Intelligence)

Narrow AI, also known as Weak AI, is designed to perform a specific task or a limited set of related tasks.

This is the type of AI that exists today and is used in almost every industry.

Examples include:

- Chatbots
- Language translation
- Email spam filters
- Face recognition
- Voice assistants
- Recommendation systems
- AI writing assistants
- AI image generators
- AI coding assistants

Although these systems can perform their assigned tasks extremely well, they cannot think beyond the functions they were designed to perform.

---

## General AI (Artificial General Intelligence)

Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) refers to a theoretical AI system capable of performing any intellectual task that a human being can perform.

An AGI would be able to:

- Learn new skills independently
- Reason through unfamiliar problems
- Adapt to different situations
- Transfer knowledge between different subjects
- Make complex decisions across many fields

At present, General AI has **not** been achieved. It remains an active area of research.

---

## Super Artificial Intelligence

Super Artificial Intelligence (ASI) describes a hypothetical future AI that would surpass human intelligence in nearly every field.

An ASI could theoretically outperform humans in areas such as:

- Scientific research
- Engineering
- Medicine
- Mathematics
- Creativity
- Strategic planning
- Problem-solving

Super AI does not currently exist and remains a concept discussed by researchers, scientists and technology leaders.

---

## Reactive Machines

Reactive Machines are the simplest form of AI.

These systems:

- Respond only to current information.
- Do not remember previous interactions.
- Cannot learn from past experiences.

They are designed to react to a specific input with a predefined response.

Examples include some game-playing systems and simple automated machines.

---

## Limited Memory AI

Most modern AI systems belong to this category.

Limited Memory AI can use previous information for a short period to improve its decisions.

Examples include:

- Self-driving vehicle technology
- Modern conversational AI assistants
- Fraud detection systems
- Recommendation engines
- Navigation systems

These systems analyze previous data while generating responses but do not possess long-term human-like memory.

---

## Theory of Mind AI

Theory of Mind AI is a research concept that focuses on creating AI capable of understanding human emotions, intentions and beliefs.

Such systems would ideally understand:

- Human emotions
- Social interactions
- Intentions
- Motivation
- Context

Although research is progressing, this level of AI has not yet been achieved.

---

## Self-Aware AI

Self-Aware AI represents the most advanced theoretical stage of Artificial Intelligence.

A self-aware AI would possess:

- Self-consciousness
- Independent awareness
- Personal understanding of its own existence

At present, no self-aware AI exists.

---

## Which Type of AI Exists Today?

Almost every AI application currently available belongs to the Narrow AI category.

Examples include:

- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Google Gemini
- Microsoft Copilot
- Perplexity AI
- Siri
- Alexa
- Google Assistant

These systems are highly capable within their intended functions but are not examples of General AI.

---

## Why This Matters

Understanding the different types of AI helps users:

- Set realistic expectations.
- Recognize current AI limitations.
- Understand future possibilities.
- Use AI more effectively and responsibly.

Knowing what today's AI can and cannot do allows users to make better decisions when using AI tools.

---

## Key Takeaways

After completing this lesson, you should understand that:

- Most AI systems today are examples of Narrow AI.
- General AI and Super AI remain theoretical concepts.
- Different AI categories have different capabilities.
- Current AI systems are powerful but specialized.
- Understanding AI types helps users use AI more effectively and responsibly.
