# Common Prompting Mistakes

## Introduction

Even the most advanced AI systems can produce poor results when given poor instructions.

Many beginners believe that AI is the problem when they receive an unsatisfactory response. In reality, the issue is often the prompt itself.

Fortunately, most prompting mistakes are easy to avoid. By understanding these common errors and learning how to correct them, you can consistently achieve better results with AI.

---

## Mistake 1: Being Too Vague

One of the most common mistakes is asking AI to do something without providing enough information.

For example:

> Tell me about business.

This prompt is too broad. The AI doesn't know:

- What type of business?
- Who is the audience?
- How detailed should the explanation be?
- What format is expected?

A better prompt would be:

> Explain how small businesses can attract customers using digital marketing. Write for beginners and include five practical tips.

The more specific your request, the more useful the response will be.

---

## Mistake 2: Not Providing Enough Context

AI performs better when it understands your situation.

Imagine asking:

> Write an email.

The AI doesn't know:

- Who the email is for.
- Why you are writing.
- The tone you want.
- The outcome you expect.

A better prompt would be:

> Write a professional email thanking a client for purchasing our software and inviting them to schedule a free onboarding session.

Providing context allows AI to generate responses that are more accurate and relevant.

---

## Mistake 3: Asking Too Many Unrelated Questions at Once

Sometimes users try to complete several unrelated tasks in a single prompt.

For example:

> Explain blockchain, write Python code, create a business plan, design a logo and translate everything into French.

Although AI may attempt to answer everything, the quality often decreases because the request is too broad.

Instead, divide large projects into smaller, focused prompts.

Working step by step usually produces much better results.

---

## Mistake 4: Forgetting to Specify the Audience

Different audiences require different explanations.

Compare these prompts:

> Explain Artificial Intelligence.

versus

> Explain Artificial Intelligence to secondary school students using simple language and everyday examples.

The second prompt gives AI enough information to adjust its vocabulary and level of detail.

Always think about who will read the final output.

---

## Mistake 5: Ignoring the Desired Format

AI can present information in many different ways.

If you don't specify the format, the output may not match your expectations.

Examples of formats include:

- Bullet points
- Numbered lists
- Tables
- Reports
- Articles
- Emails
- Presentations
- Checklists

For example:

> Compare Bitcoin and Ethereum using a table with six comparison points.

Clearly defining the format saves time and reduces the need for revisions.

---

## Mistake 6: Accepting the First Response Without Reviewing It

AI-generated content should always be reviewed before use.

Check for:

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Grammar
- Logical flow
- Missing information
- Outdated facts

If something isn't quite right, ask AI to improve it.

Examples include:

- Expand this section.
- Simplify the language.
- Add more examples.
- Rewrite professionally.
- Shorten the response.

Good prompting is often an iterative process.

---

## Mistake 7: Assuming AI Is Always Correct

Artificial Intelligence is a powerful assistant, but it is not perfect.

Sometimes AI may:

- Misinterpret a question.
- Generate incorrect facts.
- Omit important details.
- Produce outdated information.
- Create convincing but inaccurate answers.

For important decisions involving health, finance, law or business, always verify information using reliable sources or qualified professionals.

---

## Mistake 8: Using AI Without Ethical Considerations

AI should be used responsibly.

Avoid using AI to:

- Spread misinformation.
- Plagiarize someone else's work.
- Generate deceptive content.
- Violate privacy.
- Engage in illegal or unethical activities.

Responsible AI use builds trust and helps ensure that AI benefits society.

---

## How to Improve Your Prompts

Before submitting any prompt, ask yourself:

- Is my objective clear?
- Have I provided enough context?
- Did I specify the audience?
- Did I choose the desired format?
- Have I explained the tone?
- Did I include any important constraints?

If the answer is "yes" to all of these questions, your prompt is likely to produce much better results.

---

## Best Practices

Successful AI users develop good prompting habits.

Remember to:

- Be clear and specific.
- Provide context.
- Define the audience.
- Specify the output format.
- Review the results.
- Refine your prompts when necessary.
- Verify important information.

Prompt engineering is a skill that improves with regular practice.

---

## Key Takeaways

After completing this lesson, you should understand that:

- Most poor AI responses are caused by unclear prompts.
- Specific, detailed prompts produce better results.
- Large tasks should be broken into smaller steps.
- AI-generated information should always be reviewed and verified.
- Prompt engineering is an ongoing process of testing, refining and improving your instructions.
