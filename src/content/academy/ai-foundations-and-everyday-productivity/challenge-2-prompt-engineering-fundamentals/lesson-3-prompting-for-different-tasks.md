# Prompting for Different Tasks

## Introduction

Not all prompts are written the same way.

A prompt that works well for writing a blog article may not be suitable for generating computer code, creating an image, analyzing data or planning a business strategy.

Learning how to adapt your prompts for different tasks allows you to get the best results from AI, regardless of the tool you are using.

In this lesson, you will explore how to structure prompts for some of the most common real-world applications of Artificial Intelligence.

---

## Writing and Content Creation

AI is widely used for creating written content.

Examples include:

- Blog articles
- Newsletters
- Business proposals
- Reports
- Emails
- Product descriptions
- Social media posts
- Scripts
- Speeches

A good writing prompt should include:

- The topic
- The target audience
- The writing style
- The desired length
- Any important points to include

Example:

> Write a 700-word beginner-friendly article explaining blockchain technology to university students. Use simple language, practical examples and conclude with five key takeaways.

The more details you provide, the more useful the output is likely to be.

---

## Research and Learning

AI can help explain complex subjects, summarize information and organize knowledge.

Examples include:

- Explaining difficult concepts
- Summarizing textbooks
- Creating study notes
- Preparing revision questions
- Comparing different ideas
- Generating practice quizzes

Example:

> Explain cloud computing to a beginner using simple language. Include real-life examples, advantages, disadvantages and a short summary.

Always verify important facts using reliable sources, especially for academic or professional work.

---

## Programming and Software Development

Developers frequently use AI to:

- Write code
- Debug errors
- Explain programming concepts
- Optimize existing code
- Convert code between programming languages
- Generate documentation

A good programming prompt should include:

- The programming language
- The objective
- Existing code (if applicable)
- Any requirements or constraints

Example:

> Write a JavaScript function that sorts an array of numbers in ascending order. Include comments explaining each step.

Providing the full error message or relevant code often leads to better solutions.

---

## Business and Entrepreneurship

Business professionals use AI for planning, analysis and decision-making.

Common tasks include:

- Business plans
- SWOT analysis
- Marketing strategies
- Financial projections
- Customer communication
- Product ideas
- Meeting summaries

Example:

> Create a marketing strategy for a new online clothing store targeting university students with a limited advertising budget.

Including details about your business helps AI produce more relevant recommendations.

---

## Data Analysis

AI can help organize and interpret data.

Examples include:

- Identifying trends
- Explaining charts
- Comparing statistics
- Summarizing survey results
- Creating reports

Example:

> Analyze these monthly sales figures and identify the three main trends. Suggest possible reasons for each trend.

Always review AI-generated analysis before making important business decisions.

---

## Image Generation

Modern AI tools can generate high-quality images from written descriptions.

Image prompts should describe:

- The subject
- The environment
- The style
- The lighting
- The colors
- The camera angle or perspective
- The desired quality

Example:

> Create a futuristic smart city at sunset with modern skyscrapers, flying vehicles and lush green parks. Use a cinematic style with realistic lighting and high detail.

The more descriptive the prompt, the more closely the generated image will match your vision.

---

## Video Creation

AI can also generate or assist with video production.

Prompts may include:

- Story ideas
- Scene descriptions
- Camera movements
- Character actions
- Narration
- Visual style
- Video duration

Example:

> Create a 60-second promotional video introducing a new fitness application. Use energetic visuals, smooth transitions and motivational narration.

Breaking long videos into multiple scenes often produces better results.

---

## Translation and Language Learning

AI is useful for multilingual communication.

Examples include:

- Translating documents
- Learning vocabulary
- Practicing conversations
- Improving grammar
- Correcting writing

Example:

> Translate this business email from English into French while maintaining a professional tone.

AI can also explain why certain words or phrases are used, making it a valuable language-learning companion.

---

## Creative Brainstorming

AI is an excellent brainstorming partner.

It can generate:

- Business ideas
- Product names
- Slogans
- Marketing campaigns
- Book titles
- Video concepts
- Event themes
- Startup ideas

Example:

> Suggest 20 unique business ideas that combine Artificial Intelligence and agriculture in Africa.

The quality of brainstorming often improves when you clearly define the industry, audience and objectives.

---

## Combining Multiple Tasks

One of AI's greatest strengths is handling multiple related tasks within a single conversation.

For example, AI can:

1. Research a topic.
2. Summarize the findings.
3. Create a presentation.
4. Write a speech.
5. Design social media posts.
6. Suggest marketing ideas.

Keeping related tasks within the same conversation allows the AI to maintain context and produce more consistent results.

---

## Best Practices

When prompting for different tasks:

- Clearly describe the task.
- Provide relevant background information.
- Specify the desired output.
- Mention any important constraints.
- Review and refine the results when necessary.

Different tasks require different prompting styles, but clarity and context remain the keys to success.

---

## Key Takeaways

After completing this lesson, you should understand that:

- Different AI tasks require different prompting approaches.
- Clear, detailed prompts generally produce better results.
- AI can assist with writing, research, coding, business, image generation, video creation and many other activities.
- Providing context improves the quality and relevance of AI responses.
- Effective prompting is a flexible skill that can be adapted to almost any professional or personal task.
