# Writing Better Prompts

## Introduction

One of the biggest differences between beginners and experienced AI users is not the AI tool they use—it's how they communicate with it.

Artificial Intelligence can only respond to the information it receives. If your instructions are unclear, incomplete or too general, the results are often disappointing. On the other hand, well-written prompts help AI generate responses that are more accurate, useful and relevant.

Learning how to write better prompts is one of the fastest ways to improve your productivity with AI.

---

## Characteristics of a Good Prompt

A good prompt usually contains enough information for the AI to clearly understand the task.

Strong prompts are generally:

- Clear
- Specific
- Relevant
- Well-structured
- Easy to understand

Avoid vague or confusing instructions whenever possible.

---

## Be Clear About Your Objective

Start by telling the AI exactly what you want.

Instead of writing:

> Write something about business.

Write:

> Write a beginner-friendly article explaining how small businesses can use social media to attract customers.

The second prompt gives the AI a much clearer objective.

---

## Provide Context

Context helps AI better understand your situation.

Imagine asking:

> Write an email.

The AI doesn't know:

- Who the email is for.
- Why you're writing it.
- The tone you want.
- The goal of the message.

A better prompt would be:

> Write a professional email to a client thanking them for attending our meeting and summarizing the agreed next steps.

Providing context almost always improves the quality of the response.

---

## Specify the Audience

Different audiences require different writing styles.

For example:

Instead of writing:

> Explain Artificial Intelligence.

Try:

> Explain Artificial Intelligence to a 12-year-old student using simple language and practical examples.

Or:

> Explain Artificial Intelligence to business executives considering AI adoption.

The audience changes both the vocabulary and the depth of the explanation.

---

## Choose the Desired Format

AI can produce information in many different formats.

Examples include:

- Bullet points
- Numbered lists
- Tables
- Articles
- Reports
- Emails
- Social media posts
- Business proposals
- Presentations
- Checklists

For example:

> Create a table comparing Bitcoin and Ethereum.

Or:

> Write five bullet points summarizing this article.

Specifying the format saves time and produces more organized results.

---

## Define the Length

Tell the AI how long the response should be.

Examples:

- 100 words
- 500 words
- One paragraph
- Five bullet points
- A two-page report
- A one-minute speech

Without guidance, AI may produce responses that are much longer or shorter than you expected.

---

## Specify the Tone

The tone determines how the response sounds.

Examples include:

- Professional
- Friendly
- Formal
- Informal
- Persuasive
- Educational
- Motivational
- Technical
- Conversational

For example:

> Write a friendly welcome email for new customers.

Or:

> Write a formal business proposal for investors.

---

## Break Complex Tasks into Smaller Steps

Large requests can often be improved by dividing them into smaller tasks.

Instead of asking:

> Build an entire business plan.

Ask AI to work step by step.

For example:

1. Create the executive summary.
2. Write the market analysis.
3. Identify competitors.
4. Develop the marketing strategy.
5. Prepare financial projections.

Breaking complex tasks into smaller prompts often produces higher-quality results.

---

## Review and Refine

Your first prompt does not have to be perfect.

Experienced AI users often improve their prompts after reviewing the first response.

You can ask AI to:

- Simplify the explanation.
- Add more examples.
- Rewrite professionally.
- Shorten the response.
- Expand specific sections.
- Change the writing style.
- Correct mistakes.

Prompt engineering is an iterative process. Small improvements to a prompt can lead to significantly better results.

---

## Common Mistakes

Avoid these common prompting mistakes:

- Asking vague questions.
- Providing too little information.
- Giving conflicting instructions.
- Forgetting to specify the audience.
- Forgetting to define the format.
- Expecting AI to know missing details.
- Accepting the first response without reviewing it.

Most poor AI results are caused by poor prompts rather than poor AI.

---

## Best Practices

When writing prompts, remember to:

- State your objective clearly.
- Provide enough context.
- Identify the intended audience.
- Specify the desired format.
- Define the tone.
- Indicate the preferred length.
- Review and improve the results when necessary.

Following these practices will consistently produce better AI responses.

---

## Key Takeaways

After completing this lesson, you should understand that:

- Clear prompts produce better AI responses.
- Context helps AI understand your request.
- Specifying the audience improves relevance.
- Defining the format, tone and length produces more useful outputs.
- Prompt engineering is an iterative process that improves through refinement and practice.
