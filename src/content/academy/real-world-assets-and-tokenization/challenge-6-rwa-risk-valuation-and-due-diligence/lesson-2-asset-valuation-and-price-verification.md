# Asset Valuation and Price Verification

Before any Real-World Asset (RWA) is tokenized or offered to investors, its value should be carefully determined. Accurate valuation helps ensure fairness, transparency and informed decision-making.

Valuation is not a one-time activity. Asset values can change over time due to market conditions, economic trends and the physical condition of the asset. Regular price verification helps maintain confidence in the project.

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

## 1. What Is Asset Valuation?

Asset valuation is the process of estimating the current value of an asset.

The valuation provides a reference point for:

- Investment decisions.
- Token pricing.
- Financial reporting.
- Fundraising.
- Risk assessment.

An inaccurate valuation may mislead investors and affect the success of a project.

---

## 2. Why Valuation Matters

A reliable valuation helps:

- Determine a fair token price.
- Improve investor confidence.
- Support financial transparency.
- Assist with regulatory compliance.
- Reduce disputes over asset value.

Professional valuation is an important part of responsible RWA projects.

---

## 3. Common Valuation Methods

Different assets require different valuation approaches.

### Market Comparison

The asset is compared with similar assets that have recently been bought or sold.

Commonly used for:

- Residential property.
- Commercial buildings.
- Land.

---

### Income Approach

The asset is valued based on the income it is expected to generate.

Examples include:

- Rental properties.
- Office buildings.
- Hotels.
- Business assets.

---

### Cost Approach

The value is estimated based on the cost of replacing or rebuilding the asset, taking depreciation into account.

Often used for:

- Industrial facilities.
- Specialized equipment.
- Infrastructure.

---

## 4. Price Verification

Valuation should not rely on assumptions alone.

Projects often verify prices using:

- Independent valuation firms.
- Certified appraisers.
- Market reports.
- Financial statements.
- Industry data.
- Professional audits.

Independent verification increases credibility.

---

## 5. Factors That Affect Asset Value

Asset values may change due to:

- Supply and demand.
- Economic conditions.
- Interest rates.
- Inflation.
- Property condition.
- Business performance.
- Commodity prices.
- Government policies.

These factors should be reviewed regularly throughout the life of the project.

---

## 6. Ongoing Valuation

Many RWA projects perform periodic revaluations.

Reasons include:

- Market price changes.
- Asset improvements.
- Economic developments.
- Regulatory requirements.
- Financial reporting.

Updated valuations help investors make informed decisions based on current information.

---

## Practical Example

A company owns a commercial office building.

Before tokenization:

- An independent valuation firm estimates the property's value at **$8 million**.
- The project uses this valuation to determine the number of tokens to issue.
- Annual property reviews are scheduled to monitor changes in market value.
- Updated valuation reports are shared with investors.

This process improves transparency and supports fair pricing.
