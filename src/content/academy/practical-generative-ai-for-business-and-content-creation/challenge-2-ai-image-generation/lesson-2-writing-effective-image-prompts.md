# Writing Effective Image Prompts

The quality of an AI-generated image depends largely on the quality of the prompt. A well-written prompt gives the AI clear instructions about what to create.

The more specific your prompt, the more likely the AI will generate the image you have in mind.

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Write clear image prompts.
- Include important details in your prompts.
- Improve prompts to get better results.
- Avoid common prompting mistakes.

---

## 1. The Structure of a Good Prompt

A good image prompt normally answers these questions:

- What is the subject?
- Where is the scene?
- What style is desired?
- What colours should be used?
- What lighting is required?
- What camera angle or perspective is preferred?

Example:

> Create a confident young entrepreneur working inside a modern office, wearing business attire, with futuristic AI holograms, blue and gold colour theme, soft natural lighting, ultra-realistic photography.

---

## 2. Be Specific

Avoid very short prompts.

Instead of:

> Create a businessman.

Use:

> Create a confident African businessman in a modern office wearing a navy-blue suit, working on a laptop with AI holographic charts floating above the desk. Soft daylight, realistic photography.

Specific prompts usually produce much better results.

---

## 3. Describe the Style

You can tell the AI what artistic style you want.

Examples include:

- Photorealistic
- Cinematic
- Watercolour
- Oil painting
- Digital art
- 3D render
- Cartoon
- Anime
- Sketch

Example:

> Create a cinematic portrait of a female software engineer working with futuristic AI technology.

---

## 4. Add Lighting and Colours

Lighting changes the mood of an image.

Examples include:

- Soft daylight
- Golden hour
- Studio lighting
- Neon lighting
- Sunset
- Dramatic shadows

Colours also influence the overall appearance.

Example:

> Use a premium blue and gold colour palette with warm studio lighting.

---

## 5. Improve Your Prompt

If the first image is not satisfactory, improve the prompt instead of starting over.

For example:

Original prompt:

> Create a coffee shop.

Improved prompt:

> Create a luxury modern coffee shop with large glass windows, wooden furniture, indoor plants, smiling customers, warm lighting and realistic photography.

Small improvements often produce significantly better results.

---

## Common Mistakes

Avoid prompts that are:

- Too short
- Too vague
- Missing important details
- Contradictory
- Overly complicated

A clear prompt is usually more effective than an extremely long one.

---

## Practical Exercise

Generate two images.

### Prompt 1

> Create a technology conference.

### Prompt 2

> Create a modern international technology conference with diverse professionals, large digital presentation screens, AI demonstrations, premium blue lighting, realistic photography and a futuristic atmosphere.

Compare both images and identify why the second prompt produces a better result.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Clear prompts produce better images.
- Include the subject, style, colours and lighting.
- Be specific rather than vague.
- Improve prompts through experimentation.
- Small prompt changes can make a big difference.
