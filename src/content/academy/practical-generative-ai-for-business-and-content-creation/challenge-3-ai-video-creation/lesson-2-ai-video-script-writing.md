# AI Video Script Writing

A great video starts with a great script. Before generating visuals, AI needs clear instructions about what should happen in the video. A well-written script helps AI produce videos that are engaging, organized and easy to understand.

Whether you're creating a product advertisement, an educational lesson or a social media video, the script is the foundation of the final result.

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Understand the importance of video scripts.
- Structure a simple AI video script.
- Write prompts for AI video generators.
- Improve AI-generated scripts.

---

## 1. Why Scripts Matter

A script tells the AI:

- What the video is about.
- Who the audience is.
- What should happen in each scene.
- What should be spoken.
- What action viewers should take.

Without a script, videos may feel disorganized or unclear.

---

## 2. Basic Video Structure

A simple video usually contains:

### Introduction

Capture attention within the first few seconds.

Example:

> "Did you know AI can save your business hours every week?"

---

### Main Content

Present the key information clearly.

Keep each point short and focused.

---

### Conclusion

End with a clear summary and call to action.

Example:

> "Start using AI today and transform the way you work."

---

## 3. Writing Better AI Prompts

Instead of writing:

> Create a business video.

Use:

> Create a 30-second promotional video introducing an AI learning platform for entrepreneurs. Use a professional presenter, modern office background, upbeat music and finish with a call to action encouraging viewers to register.

The more details you provide, the better the AI understands your request.

---

## 4. Keep It Simple

Most short videos communicate one main idea.

Avoid trying to explain too many topics in a single video.

One message is usually more memorable than several unrelated ideas.

---

## 5. Review the Script

Before generating the video, check:

- Is the message clear?
- Is it easy to understand?
- Is the script too long?
- Does it match the audience?
- Does it include a call to action?

Small improvements can greatly improve the final video.

---

## Practical Exercise

Write a script for a **30-second promotional video** about a product, service or online course.

Include:

- A strong opening.
- Two or three key benefits.
- A closing call to action.

Then ask an AI video tool to generate the video using your script.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Every successful AI video begins with a clear script.
- Good scripts have an introduction, main content and conclusion.
- Detailed prompts produce better videos.
- Short, focused scripts are usually more effective.
- Always review the script before generating the final video.
