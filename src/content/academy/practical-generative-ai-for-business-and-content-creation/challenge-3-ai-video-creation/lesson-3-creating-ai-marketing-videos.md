# Creating AI Marketing Videos

Marketing videos are one of the most effective ways to promote products, services and businesses. AI makes it possible to create professional marketing videos quickly, even without advanced video editing skills.

A successful marketing video should capture attention, communicate value and encourage viewers to take action.

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Create AI-generated marketing videos.
- Structure a simple promotional video.
- Highlight product or service benefits effectively.
- Produce videos that encourage customer action.

---

## 1. Know Your Objective

Before creating a marketing video, ask yourself:

- What am I promoting?
- Who is my audience?
- What action do I want viewers to take?

A clear objective helps the AI generate more focused content.

---

## 2. Structure Your Video

A simple marketing video can follow this format:

### Hook

Capture attention immediately.

Example:

> "Still spending hours creating content?"

---

### Problem

Show the challenge your audience faces.

Example:

> "Many businesses struggle to create quality marketing materials consistently."

---

### Solution

Present your product or service.

Example:

> "Our AI platform helps you create professional content in minutes."

---

### Benefits

Highlight the main advantages.

Examples:

- Saves time
- Easy to use
- Professional results
- Affordable
- Increases productivity

---

### Call to Action

Tell viewers what to do next.

Examples:

- Register today.
- Visit our website.
- Download the app.
- Book a free consultation.
- Learn more.

---

## 3. Use Strong Visual Descriptions

When writing your AI prompt, describe the visuals clearly.

Example:

> Create a 45-second promotional video showing entrepreneurs working with AI tools inside a modern office. Use bright lighting, smooth transitions, professional typography and a premium blue-and-gold colour theme.

Clear visual instructions improve the final result.

---

## 4. Keep Videos Short

Short videos usually perform better on social media.

Recommended lengths:

- 15–30 seconds for advertisements.
- 30–60 seconds for product introductions.
- 1–3 minutes for educational or promotional content.

Focus on one main message instead of covering too many topics.

---

## 5. Review Before Publishing

Before publishing your video, check:

- Is the message clear?
- Does the video match your brand?
- Are the visuals consistent?
- Is the voiceover accurate?
- Is the call to action easy to understand?

Small improvements can significantly increase the effectiveness of the video.

---

## Practical Exercise

Create a **45-second promotional video** for a business, product or online course.

Include:

- A strong opening hook.
- The problem being solved.
- Three key benefits.
- A professional call to action.

Generate the video using an AI video tool and review the final result.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Marketing videos should have a clear objective.
- A simple structure improves viewer engagement.
- Strong visual descriptions produce better AI videos.
- Short videos are often more effective on social media.
- Always review AI-generated videos before publishing.
