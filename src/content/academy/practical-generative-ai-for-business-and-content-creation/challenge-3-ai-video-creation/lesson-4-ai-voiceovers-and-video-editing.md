# AI Voiceovers & Video Editing

AI can now generate natural-sounding voiceovers and assist with video editing. This allows businesses, educators and content creators to produce professional videos without expensive equipment or advanced editing skills.

By combining AI-generated visuals, voiceovers and editing tools, you can create polished videos in much less time.

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Understand AI voice generation.
- Use AI tools to improve video editing.
- Choose an appropriate voice for your audience.
- Review AI-generated videos before publishing.

---

## 1. AI Voiceovers

AI voice tools convert written text into spoken audio.

They can be used for:

- Product advertisements
- Educational lessons
- Business presentations
- YouTube videos
- Social media content
- Customer tutorials

Many AI platforms allow you to choose different voices, accents and speaking styles.

---

## 2. Choosing the Right Voice

The voice should match the purpose of the video.

Examples:

- Professional for business presentations.
- Friendly for educational content.
- Energetic for product advertisements.
- Calm for tutorials and training videos.

A suitable voice improves the viewer's experience.

---

## 3. AI Video Editing

AI can simplify many editing tasks.

Examples include:

- Automatic subtitles
- Background music suggestions
- Scene transitions
- Noise reduction
- Video trimming
- Background removal
- Colour enhancement
- Automatic captions

These features reduce editing time while improving video quality.

---

## 4. Add Captions

Many people watch videos without sound.

Captions help by:

- Improving accessibility.
- Increasing viewer engagement.
- Making videos easier to understand.
- Helping viewers follow the content in noisy environments.

Always review captions for spelling and accuracy before publishing.

---

## 5. Review the Final Video

Before publishing, check:

- Is the voice clear?
- Do the visuals match the narration?
- Are subtitles accurate?
- Is the pacing comfortable?
- Does the video end with a clear call to action?

Small improvements can make your video more professional.

---

## Practical Exercise

Create a **30–60 second AI video** about a business, product or educational topic.

Include:

- AI-generated visuals.
- An AI voiceover.
- Automatic captions.
- A closing call to action.

Review the final video and identify at least two improvements you could make before publishing.

---

## Key Takeaways

- AI can generate realistic voiceovers from text.
- AI editing tools speed up video production.
- Choosing the right voice improves communication.
- Captions increase accessibility and engagement.
- Always review the final video before sharing it.
