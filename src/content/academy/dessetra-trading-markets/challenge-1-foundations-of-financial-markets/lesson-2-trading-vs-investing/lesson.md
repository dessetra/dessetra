# Setting Up MT5 & TradingView

Knowing Forex terminology is not enough.

A trader must also know how to use the tools through which markets are analysed and trades are executed.

In this course, we will primarily use:

* **MetaTrader 5 (MT5)** for trade execution and account monitoring.
* **TradingView** for chart analysis and market preparation.

Before risking real money, you should become comfortable navigating these tools using a **demo trading environment**.

---

## Why Start With a Demo Account?

A demo account allows you to practise trading using virtual funds instead of risking your real capital.

It gives you an opportunity to learn:

* how the trading platform works
* how currency pairs are displayed
* how charts behave
* how orders are placed
* how trades are monitored
* how Stop Loss and Take Profit work
* how profit and loss change as price moves

Treat your demo account seriously.

Do not think:

> â€œIt is fake money, so it does not matter.â€

The purpose of demo trading is to practise the same process and discipline you would eventually apply to real capital.

For the practical activities in this course, **use a demo account unless a lesson specifically states otherwise.**

---

## Understanding the Role of MT5

**MetaTrader 5 (MT5)** is a trading platform commonly provided by Forex and CFD brokers.

It can be used to:

* view market prices
* open charts
* monitor different symbols
* change chart timeframes
* place and manage orders
* set Stop Loss and Take Profit
* monitor account balance and equity
* review open positions
* review trading history

Your exact list of available instruments depends on your broker.

This means the same instrument may sometimes appear differently across brokers.

For example, one broker may display:

**XAUUSD**

while another may use a variation such as:

**XAUUSDm**

or another broker-specific suffix.

Always confirm the exact symbol offered by your broker.

If you do not already have MT5, install it from an official source or use the version provided by your selected broker.

## Understanding Timeframes

A timeframe determines how much trading activity each candle represents.

For example:

**M5 = 5 Minutes**

Each candle represents five minutes of price activity.

**M15 = 15 Minutes**

Each candle represents fifteen minutes.

**H1 = 1 Hour**

Each candle represents one hour.

**H4 = 4 Hours**

Each candle represents four hours.

The same market can look very different depending on the timeframe being viewed.

A market may appear bullish on one timeframe while showing a short-term bearish movement on another.

This is why traders must always know which timeframe they are analysing.

## Understanding TradingView

**TradingView** is a charting and market-analysis platform.

While MT5 will be particularly important for executing and monitoring demo trades, TradingView will help us perform clearer visual analysis.

You can use TradingView to:

* search for instruments
* open charts
* change timeframes
* use candlestick charts
* draw support and resistance
* mark market structure
* add indicators
* save chart layouts
* annotate trading ideas

You do not need advanced TradingView features yet.

Your first goal is simply to become comfortable opening and navigating a chart.

Different data providers and brokers may display small differences in quotes.

Your focus at this stage is learning how to navigate and analyse the chart.

## MT5 vs TradingView

For this course, think of the two platforms this way:

| MT5                       | TradingView                  |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Trade execution           | Market analysis              |
| Demo account management   | Detailed chart study         |
| Open and close positions  | Drawing and annotation       |
| Stop Loss and Take Profit | Market structure analysis    |
| Trade history             | Planning potential setups    |
| Account monitoring        | Technical-analysis workspace |

This does not mean MT5 cannot perform technical analysis or TradingView cannot support broker-connected trading.

It simply defines **how we will use them during this course**.
